Locally Owned & Family Run

Frank Harley Custom Woodworking is a proud family-run business based right here in Indianapolis. What started in our garage has grown into a fully capable precision workshop. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to create one-of-a-kind pieces.

Our shop features advanced equipment, including two large-format 1500mm x 1500mm CNC machines (a WorkBee 1515 and an Oxman by Bulkman 3D) and a specialized CNC Lathe. This powerful setup allows us to handle everything from intricate 3D carvings to large-scale custom furniture with exact precision.
